section 01 · The Parties
Who is who, and what each brings

Not a vendor. Not a client. A flagship.

David put it best on our call: we are helping one another — you have your tools, and we are giving you your first school platform. That is the deal, exactly. StarHub Studios PBC brings the tools: the platform, the three portals, growth operations, the storytelling engine, and O.M.G. and S.O.S. — two programmes built before we met, included from day one and not held back as an upsell. Wonderstorm Academy — David Yarbrough and Holly Yarbrough — brings the school, the method, the families, and the thing no amount of engineering produces: the first school. The proving ground, the reference, the case every school after this one gets shown. Neither side is buying from the other. One side brings a working school; the other brings the system that lets it travel.

◆ The discipline underneath every clause

Your tuition ⊥ revenue from new digital lines ⊥ revenue from other schools. Three ledgers, kept apart, each with its own split. We never take a share of a dollar we had nothing to do with, and we take no share of your tuition at any size. Where something is left open in this document, it is open on purpose, and it says who closes it.

section 02 · What This Is, and What It Is Not
Three things it could have been — and the one it is

A revenue partnership. Not an investment, not a sale.

What it is NOT Why that matters to you
Not an equity deal. StarHub takes no ownership interest in Wonderstorm Academy — none at formation, none on recovery, none ever, absent a separate agreement you would have to sign. Your cap table is untouched. Nothing here dilutes you, and nothing here gives us a vote in your school.
Not a licence of your method. The WonderStorm method, curriculum, brand and community are yours. We operate a platform; we do not acquire what runs on it. If this partnership ends, the method leaves with you — intact and unencumbered.
Not an invoice. There is no bill, no retainer due on a date, and no check to write for the build. If the system never generates a dollar, you never owe one. That is the whole shape of the deal.

What it IS: a defined commercial partnership in which StarHub carries the cost of a platform it has already delivered, and recovers that cost only out of revenue the platform itself creates — then shares in continued growth for as long as it is actively producing it.

section 04 · The Commercial Terms
Every dollar, and whose it is

We share what we build. We take nothing from your students.

The platform's conservative market replacement value exceeds $250,000. Wonderstorm's founding-partner principal is fixed at $50,000 — a deep founding discount, and a number that does not move. It carries no deferral uplift: no percentage is added for being paid later, whatever our standard rate becomes. It is recovered from our half of the two streams below and from nothing else — so if those streams never produce, it is never recovered, and you are never billed for it. That is what deferred against revenue honestly means.

The three revenue ledgers

Revenue stream Whose it is The split
Tuition — every student, the ones you have and the ones who come next Yours entirely 100% Wonderstorm. We never touch it. Not a percentage, not a recovery cap, not a share of margin — nothing. The school is the company you built, and enrolling a child is not a revenue stream we created.
New digital streams — courses, memberships, quests Wonderstorm Digital — the joint entity, owned 50/50 by StarHub Studios and Wonderstorm 50 / 50 partners. Both ledgers below run through Wonderstorm Digital; neither side bills the other.
Platform licensing to other schools — the Acton network, and micro-schools and homeschool networks anywhere Wonderstorm Digital — our technology, your flagship story 50 / 50 partners. Your method and your proof are half of what makes it sellable, so they are half of what it earns.

Wonderstorm Digital &mdash; the entity that holds it

Term How it works
Ownership A fifty-fifty joint venture. Gheric Speiginer and Jack Polo on one side; David Yarbrough and Holly Yarbrough on the other. Each side holds 50% of the equity. Neither side controls it alone.
What the entity owns Everything built for and by the venture — the Wonderstorm Digital platform, its products and its assets — is owned by Wonderstorm Digital itself, not by either side individually. What each side brought with it stays that side’s own and is licensed in for the venture’s use: Wonderstorm’s method, curriculum and brand remain Wonderstorm’s; StarHub’s underlying engine, O.M.G. and S.O.S. remain StarHub’s.
Profit Fifty-fifty equity is the revenue split — there is no separate share to negotiate and no fee taken off the top before it.
Distributions Profits are distributed quarterly, within 30 days of quarter end, after operating costs and an agreed working-capital reserve. Money does not sit in the company by default. Retaining more than the reserve in any quarter requires both sides’ written agreement.
Transfer and dilution Neither side may sell, transfer, pledge or dilute its equity without the other’s written consent. Fifty-fifty stays fifty-fifty unless you both agree otherwise, in writing.
Operating costs Ad spend, content production, platform maintenance and support are borne by the entity from its own revenue — not invoiced to Wonderstorm, and not invoiced to StarHub.

This is why there is no monthly fee. Growth operations are not a service you buy from us; they are a cost of the company we own together, paid out of what it earns before anything is distributed to either side.

◆ What counts as a partnership dollar &mdash; defined, not deferred

Only two ledgers are shared, so only two things need defining. A digital-stream dollar is revenue from a product that did not exist before this partnership — a course, membership, quest pack or certification — sold through a checkout StarHub built and operates. A licensing dollar is revenue from another school, micro-school or homeschool network paying to use the platform or the productised method. Both are counted GROSS at the checkout, net of payment-processor fees and refunds only. Neither includes tuition, fees, donations, grants or any revenue Wonderstorm would have earned without this partnership — not reduced, not shared, not counted. Every shared dollar appears in the monthly report with its source; anything we cannot show, we do not claim.

◆ The rule we will not break

We do not take a percentage of your students. Not capped, not conditioned, not one third of anything — none. An earlier draft of this document recovered our build principal from the margin on new enrollments, and every protection we wrapped around that was answering the wrong question. The question was never whether the rate was safe. It was whether we should be in your tuition at all. We should not. You built the school; the children who walk into it are yours. We are paid from the streams we create, and only when they actually produce.

Your engagement — The World

What Terms
The build $50,000 — fixed, deferred, no uplift. Covers the platform and everything shipped with it: the public site, the Leader Operating System, the Coach and Parent portals, the core-skills programmes, the Shadow Day assessment, Phoenix, and the enrollment, tour-booking and FAQ layer. O.M.G. and S.O.S. are included from day one at no additional cost.
Growth operations No monthly fee. The parties acknowledge that the monthly operations fee of $1,500 per month has been removed from this agreement.
Beyond that scope Quoted and agreed separately, in writing, before work starts — never added to the principal. The rate card runs Launch $50,000 · Growth $90,000 · Empire from $150,000; a larger build moves up a rung rather than stretching this one.
Trying it first Sixty days to decide. Either side can walk at day 60 — no penalty, no clawback, the site stays yours.
Term Following the sixty-day trial period, the parties commit to an eighteen-month operations term. During this term, either party may terminate only for material breach. Upon completion of the eighteen months, the agreement converts to month-to-month, terminable by either party with thirty days’ written notice.

The World is the tier you chose: the method productised — curriculum licensing, the partner-school platform, family memberships and Coach certification. One number. The $50,000 is the only compensation for the build, and there is no recurring fee of any kind in this agreement.

section 06 · How This Protects You
The exits, the caps, the receipts

Every commitment here has a way out.

Protection What it means in practice
The 60-day out-clause Sixty days in, if it is not earning its keep, we part friends and the site stays yours. No penalty, no clawback, no argument.
Your students are not on the table There is no percentage, no cap, no margin share and no recovery mechanism attached to any child who enrolls. Tuition is 100% yours in every clause of this document. Nothing later in the agreement quietly reaches back into it.
The fixed principal $50,000, and it does not grow. No interest, no escalation, no deferral uplift, no fees added while it is outstanding. It is recovered only from our half of the digital-stream and licensing ledgers — if those never earn, it is never recovered and never invoiced.
The activity condition Our share of the streams we run applies only while StarHub is actively running them. We stop working, we stop sharing.
Attribution is measured, not asserted Revenue is only shared when it is instrumented end-to-end and visible in the monthly report. If we cannot show it in the numbers, we do not claim it.
Your data stays yours Family and student data is processed on your instruction, never sold, never used to train anything, and returned or destroyed at exit.
